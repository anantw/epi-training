Three things connect an order to you

Each one covers a gap the others can leave, so most orders are matched more than once.

60 days

Your referral tag

Saved when someone opens your link. It lasts 60 days on that phone and browser. If they open another Care Circle member's link before ordering, the newer link takes over.

₹100 off

Your code

Gives the family ₹100 off and credits you on any phone, at any time, even if the tag is lost. Your links apply it for them automatically.

For life

The lifetime link

After their first order through you, every future order from that customer is yours. No link or code is needed again.

When families pass it on

Families often forward things to relatives on WhatsApp. Here's what keeps your credit, and what doesn't.

    Your link goes with it, so the relative gets your tag when they open it.

  • They send a photo of your QR card

    Scanning the photo opens your link, exactly like scanning the card.

    Credited to you

    The shared link includes your tag and code. The relative sees that a nurse shared the guides, with the ₹100 code ready.

    Credited to you

What to say

You're sharing free information. The family and their doctor make the decision.

Good to say

  • "These free guides explain how nutrition supports the body's repair."
  • "Please ask your doctor whether it suits you."
  • "I'm part of Epithrive's Care Circle, so I share their guides."

Please avoid

  • Saying it heals, cures or treats a wound
  • Promising results or a healing time
  • Quoting prices or offering discounts yourself
  • Suggesting it replaces their wound care or medicines

Questions

Where do I see my orders and commission?

In your dashboard at affiliate.epithrive.in. Add your payout method there so Epithrive can pay you.

I've lost my link or QR card. What do I do?

Open your personal kit page from your welcome message on WhatsApp, or go to training.epithrive.in/welcome and open your kit from there. It rebuilds your link, your QR card and ready-made messages.

A family wants the messages to stop.

They can reply STOP to any Epithrive message. Nothing more is sent after that.

What if they buy from a pharmacy or another website?

Only orders on epithrive.in are tracked. Your links send families there, so most orders through you land in the right place.

What if they open another nurse's link later?

Before their first order, the newest link they opened takes over the 60-day tag. That's one more reason the ₹100 code matters: it identifies you whenever it's used.
