When to see a doctor or nurse

These guides are about nutrition. They are not a substitute for professional medical care. Please get the wound checked promptly if you notice any of these:

  • the wound getting larger, deeper, or more painful
  • redness, warmth, or swelling spreading around it
  • pus, fluid, or a bad smell
  • a fever, or the person feeling generally unwell
  • the skin around the wound turning dark
  • any foot wound in someone with diabetes

When in doubt, get it looked at. It is always better to ask early.
