When to see a doctor or nurse

These guides are about food. Get the wound checked promptly if you notice any of these:

  • The wound is getting larger, deeper or more painful
  • Redness, warmth or swelling is spreading around it
  • Pus, more fluid than before, or a bad smell
  • Fever, or the person seems generally unwell
  • The skin around the wound is turning dark or black
  • Any wound on the foot of someone with diabetes

When in doubt, get it looked at. Asking early is always better.
