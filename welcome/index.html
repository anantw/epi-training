Share it the right way

Epithrive® is a nutraceutical taken alongside standard wound care. How we talk about it matters, for the brand and for you.

Please do

  • Describe it as clinical nutrition that supports the body's healing response, alongside standard wound care.
  • Say it works with a doctor's wound care, not instead of it.
  • Encourage people to keep following their doctor's or nurse's advice.
  • Use the messages on this page or the wording from your training.

Please don't

  • Say it treats, cures or prevents any disease.
  • Call it a medicine, a drug or a replacement for medical treatment.
  • Quote clinical percentages or study numbers to customers.
  • Promise specific results, recovery times or income.
