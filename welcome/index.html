Share it the right way

A few simple rules that keep us all safe

Epithrive® is a nutraceutical taken alongside standard wound care. How we talk about it matters — for the brand and for you. Keep to these and you can't go wrong.

Please do

  • Describe it as clinical nutrition that supports the body's healing response as an adjunct to standard wound care.
  • Say it works alongside a doctor's wound care, not instead of it.
  • Encourage people to keep following their doctor or nurse's advice.
  • Use the ready-made messages below, or the wording from your training.

Please don't

  • Never say it will treat, cure, heal, or prevent any wound or disease.
  • Don't call it a medicine, drug, or a replacement for medical treatment.
  • Don't quote clinical percentages or study numbers to customers.
  • Don't promise specific results, recovery times, or guaranteed income.
