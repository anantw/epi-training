⚠ Before this is shown to anyone

This is a model, not a promise. Every figure here follows from the assumptions typed above. Change the active rate or the drop-off rate and the debt-free month moves by years. Nobody should be shown a specific number as something they will earn.

Most people in any affiliate programme earn little or nothing. The conservative case on this page is closer to typical than the main case. When real Care Circle data exists in BixGrow, replace this model with the actual distribution of what members earned — including everyone who earned zero.

One payment per box. Care Circle rates are the only commission on a referred box. Someone buying at distributor price earns a trade margin on those units instead — never both on the same unit.

Internal modelling tool. Not a recruitment document. Figures are arithmetic outputs of user-entered assumptions. They are not typical, expected or guaranteed earnings, and no representation is made that any person will earn these amounts. Actual results depend on qualification, active status, returns, cancellations, COD failures, GST and TDS treatment, and the programme terms in force. Presenting income projections to prospective participants engages the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules 2021 and ASCI guidance on earnings claims. Do not circulate.
